Helicopter: the joystick steers, the throttle sets your speed, and LIFT takes you up and down (middle = hold height). To take off, push LIFT up (or hold W); to land, stop, then bring LIFT down. Xbox controller: left stick steers, triggers set the throttle, right stick is lift.

Takeoff: full throttle, gently pull up at about 55 kt. Landing: line up with the runway, ease the throttle back and descend gently. The plane cushions itself near the ground. Xbox controller: left stick to fly, triggers for throttle, A brakes, B floats, Y camera, RB turbo.
