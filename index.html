      Controls & tips
      AD / joystick: steer
      Shift / Ctrl speed (stays set)
      WS up / down (let go to hold height)
      QE slide sideways   Space slow to a stop
      WS / ↑↓ climb / dive
      AD / ←→ bank left / right
      QE rudder   Space brakes
      Shift / Ctrl throttle up / down
      B floats (land on water)   T turbo (or 123)
      C camera   N big map   R restart   Esc menu
      L auto-land (within 500 ft of a landmark)
      F robot mode: WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump (hold it to fly on the jetpack)
      Grow the robot: ] bigger, [ smaller (1× to 8×). A bigger robot smashes taller buildings; at 8× even landmarks fall
      Supersizer: . makes the car (or anything) in front of you bigger, , smaller. Walk up to a supersized car and press 🚗 DRIVE (or G) to drive it; X honks for a boost
      Robot turbo: T (or 2 3) lights the rocket pack for 2× or 3× running speed and higher jumps
      Robot: X smash · G grab a car, boat or boulder, G again to throw it
      P potty: when 💩 pops up, go to the 🚽 by the helipad or runway (or sit any time you're next to one; turn prompts off in Settings)
      I invert pitch   M sound   ` collision view (outlines everything solid)

      Push LIFT up (or hold W) to take off, then raise SPEED to fly. To land, stop and bring LIFT down; the copter cushions the touchdown.

      Takeoff: full throttle, gently pull up at about 55 kt. Landing: line up with the runway, ease the throttle back and descend gently; the plane cushions itself near the ground.

      🤖 Tap the robot button (or press F) any time to turn into a giant robot. Smash buildings, stomp by jumping from high up, and throw cars. Restarting puts the city back together.

      Xbox controller: left stick to fly, triggers for throttle, A brakes, B floats, X robot (then A jump, B grab, RB smash), Y camera, RB turbo, right stick lift.
