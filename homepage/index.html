Lego Sky: Spartanburg

Fly the LEGO Technic Bush Plane over a brick-built Spartanburg, SC.

The whole city is built from real map data: 36,000 buildings, real streets, creeks and hills, all in Lego bricks. Take off from Downtown Memorial Airport, buzz Morgan Square and Wofford, and drop the boat floats to land on the river.
